How to use

  1. Upload your designed ID card as a PDF (front + optional back)
  2. Select a field from the dropdown on the left, click Draw
  3. Drag a rectangle on the card to mark where that field's data goes
  4. Repeat for all fields, then Save Template
  5. Select cards from the Generate List (left), click Generate PDF
  6. Download the data-layer PDF — merge with your template design for final cards
